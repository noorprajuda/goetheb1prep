# Social Media Strategy for Goethe B1 Prep

## 🎯 Platform Overview

### Primary Platforms

- **Facebook** - Educational content, community building
- **Instagram** - Visual content, stories, reels
- **TikTok** - Short-form educational videos
- **X (Twitter)** - Quick tips, news, engagement

## 📱 Platform-Specific Strategies

### Facebook & Instagram

**Handle:** @goetheb1prep  
**Content Mix:** 40% Educational, 30% User Success Stories, 20% Behind-the-Scenes, 10% Promotional

#### Content Pillars

1. **Educational Content**

   - German pronunciation tips
   - B1 exam preparation guides
   - Grammar explanations
   - Vocabulary building posts

2. **User Success Stories**

   - Testimonials with photos
   - Before/after speaking confidence
   - Exam pass celebrations
   - Progress tracking screenshots

3. **Behind-the-Scenes**

   - AI technology explanations
   - Team introductions
   - Development process
   - Feature updates

4. **Interactive Content**
   - Polls about German learning
   - Q&A sessions
   - Live practice sessions
   - User-generated content

#### Posting Schedule

- **Facebook:** 1-2 posts per day
- **Instagram:** 1 post + 3-5 stories per day
- **Best Times:** 7-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-9 PM (local time)

### TikTok

**Handle:** @goetheb1prep  
**Content Focus:** Quick, engaging educational videos

#### Video Types

1. **Pronunciation Challenges** (15-30 seconds)

   - "Can you pronounce this German word?"
   - "Common German pronunciation mistakes"
   - "Tongue twisters in German"

2. **B1 Exam Tips** (30-60 seconds)

   - "5 things to remember for Goethe B1"
   - "How to structure your speaking response"
   - "Common B1 speaking topics"

3. **AI Feedback Demos** (30-45 seconds)

   - "Watch our AI analyze German pronunciation"
   - "Before vs After using our simulator"
   - "Real-time feedback in action"

4. **Quick German Lessons** (15-30 seconds)
   - "German words you need to know for B1"
   - "Essential German phrases"
   - "German grammar made simple"

#### Posting Schedule

- **TikTok:** 2-3 videos per day
- **Best Times:** 6-9 AM, 12-3 PM, 7-10 PM

### X (Twitter)

**Handle:** @goetheb1prep  
**Content Focus:** Quick tips, news, community engagement

#### Tweet Types

1. **Daily German Tips**

   - "Today's German word: [word] - [pronunciation] - [meaning]"
   - "B1 Grammar tip: [rule] with examples"
   - "Common mistake: [mistake] vs [correct]"

2. **News & Updates**

   - Feature announcements
   - User success stories
   - German language learning news
   - Goethe exam updates

3. **Community Engagement**
   - Retweet user content
   - Reply to German learning questions
   - Participate in language learning discussions
   - Share relevant articles

#### Posting Schedule

- **Twitter:** 5-8 tweets per day
- **Best Times:** 8-10 AM, 12-2 PM, 5-7 PM

## 🚀 Launch Strategy

### Pre-Launch (Month 1)

- Set up all social media accounts
- Create brand assets and guidelines
- Develop content calendar
- Build initial follower base (100-500 per platform)

### Launch Phase (Month 2-3)

- Launch with educational content
- Introduce AI features gradually
- Share user testimonials
- Engage with German learning community

### Growth Phase (Month 4-6)

- Scale content production
- Launch influencer partnerships
- Introduce user-generated content campaigns
- Optimize based on analytics

### Maturity Phase (Month 7+)

- Maintain consistent posting schedule
- Focus on community building
- Expand to new platforms if needed
- Continuous optimization and growth
